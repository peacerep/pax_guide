About this comparison

How to read it

Each marker is a formal agreement in PA-X. The vertical position represents its agreement stage; it is not a measure of the quality, success, or outcome of a peace process.

Relative time begins at each process’s first recorded agreement. Calendar time shows the agreements at their recorded dates.

Similarity suggestions

Suggestions compare recorded stage trajectories, agreement count, duration, and stage reversals. They do not compare political context, parties, or outcomes.

Data and citation

This comparison uses the PA-X Peace Agreements Dataset, Version 10: 2,257 agreements in more than 170 peace processes, covering 1990 to the end of 2025.

To cite the data:

Bell, C., & Badanjak, S. (2019). Introducing PA-X: A new peace agreement database and dataset. Journal of Peace Research, 56(3), 452–466. Available at https://www.peaceagreements.org/
